# All-printed retainer concept

Supersedes screw-held clamps for this user's requirement: no screws, nuts or metal fastening hardware. Image created with the built-in image-generation tool. This is a concept sketch; do not trace the generated cross-section as production geometry. In CAD the female groove MUST capture beneath the male rail's wide head, otherwise it cannot prevent lifting. Confirm that the sliding cap can only be removed along the rail when its latch is released.

Mechanism: tray-integral guide and support ledge; separate sliding cap with integral release latch. Guide carries uplift; fixed board corner stops resist lateral motion; latch prevents cap withdrawal. Use two opposing retainers on bare board edges where headers/components allow. Parts require physical fit and retention tests. The drawing's tolerances and dimensions are starting suggestions only.

Starting values in mm: cap body18 x10; rail head6, neck4, height2; roof2.5; nominal sliding clearance0.2 per mating face; board overlap1–2. Latch starting geometry12 long,4 broad,1.2 bending thickness,0.4 hook projection,1 root fillet. Nominal snap deflection and material strain must be checked in actual CAD and print. Board thickness and board-support height must be measured. Printed latch should be oriented to avoid peeling layer interfaces; request shop review of orientation/support removal.

Material: unfilled PETG preferred; ABS alternative with suitable shop process. PLA suitable for initial fit prototype, not assumed adequate for repeated flexing in this geometry. No material alone proves flight reliability.
